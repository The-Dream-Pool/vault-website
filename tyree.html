About Tyree

The story.

Tyree Decree is an entrepreneur, CEO, and business executive building a diversified portfolio across financial services, technology, education, and hospitality.

As Co-Founder of Vault of Modern Millionaire and Founder of GoVaultFunding.com, Tyree leads a growing ecosystem focused on financial advancement, access to capital, entrepreneurship, and modern wealth-building strategies. His vision is centered on creating businesses that not only generate value, but position people with the resources, education, and infrastructure to create opportunities of their own.

Beyond his financial ventures, Tyree is Co-Owner of Las Cuatro Milpas and serves as a key executive leader behind ChartTrendz, an emerging financial technology platform designed to modernize how traders interpret and interact with the markets.

His portfolio reflects a deliberate approach to business: acquire, build, innovate, scale, and create lasting value. Across each venture, Tyree maintains a focus on strategic expansion, strong operations, technology, and long-term brand equity.

At the center of his work is a larger vision—to build an enduring portfolio of companies, develop future leaders, expand economic opportunity, and establish a legacy defined by ownership, innovation, and generational impact.
