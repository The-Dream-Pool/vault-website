About Ryan

The story.

Ryan Lewis is a licensed Georgia real estate professional, finance student, and emerging real estate development leader with a growing foundation across residential real estate, commercial real estate, investment analysis, and community development. As an agent of Keller Williams West Atlanta, Ryan brings a combination of financial knowledge, market awareness, discipline, and relationship-driven service to every client interaction.

Her experience in the industry extends beyond residential sales. Ryan has completed multiple levels of Project Destined’s Commercial Real Estate training, gained exposure to real estate private equity, and remains actively involved with organizations such as the Urban Land Institute and collegiate real estate organizations throughout Metro Atlanta. These experiences have allowed her to study the development process, capital markets, property analysis, community planning, and the many professionals required to take a real estate project from concept to completion.

Before becoming a licensed agent, Ryan served as Director of First Impression at Keller Williams West Atlanta, giving her valuable exposure to the operations of a brokerage serving hundreds of real estate professionals. She later transitioned into her career as a licensed agent while continuing to expand her knowledge of the industry through mentorship, professional organizations, investment education, and hands-on experience.

Ryan’s discipline and work ethic were also shaped through collegiate athletics. As a former Division I track and field athlete, she developed the consistency, preparation, competitiveness, and attention to detail that now influence the way she approaches business and serves her clients.

Her long-term vision reaches beyond traditional real estate sales. Ryan aspires to become a mixed-use real estate developer and ultimately lead her own development firm focused on creating communities that positively influence how people live, think, feel, and interact with their environment. She is especially passionate about the relationship between real estate, finance, psychology, wellness, and community development.

Ryan is also committed to creating opportunities for the next generation of real estate professionals and developers. Through leadership, mentorship, and entrepreneurship initiatives, she continues to build platforms designed to expose young people to careers, resources, relationships, and opportunities within real estate and business.
