About Myla

The vision.

Myla Mims is a Florida-based REALTOR®, entrepreneur, and digital creator whose work centers on real estate, ownership, and the evolving opportunities within digital entrepreneurship.

As a Real Estate Agent with Nova Real Estate Collective, Myla works with buyers navigating the path to homeownership, helping them make informed real estate decisions and identify opportunities aligned with their personal and financial goals. Her perspective on real estate is shaped not only by her professional experience, but also by her own introduction to property ownership at an early age.

Following her high school graduation, Myla received a parcel of land as a gift from her mother. At 19, she began turning a vision for her first home into reality, starting with a concept sketched on paper and working alongside her family through the homebuilding process. This experience introduced her to the relationship between land, construction, and ownership, shaping her interest in real estate beyond the traditional transaction. Today, she is continuing to expand her involvement in real estate investment, with two income-producing property projects currently in development.

Alongside her real estate career, Myla has developed a growing presence in digital media and brand partnerships. With an audience of more than 40,000 across social platforms, she has expanded her work into content creation and collaborations with brands in technology and lifestyle. Her interest in digital entrepreneurship also led her to establish The Social Suite, a community designed to help creators and entrepreneurs understand social media monetization and build income opportunities through their online presence.

Myla's work reflects a broader interest in how people can leverage what they own, what they know, and the platforms they have access to in order to create new opportunities. Whether through real estate or digital entrepreneurship, she is focused on building with intention, expanding her professional ventures, and creating pathways toward ownership and long-term growth.
